Collision repair in Whitby

Accident damage on cars, trucks and SUVs, repaired at 105 Ash St in downtown Whitby.

Collision repair is fixing the body, panels, bumpers and paint of a vehicle after an accident so it looks and works the way it did before. Halif Auto Repair and Collision repairs collision damage on cars, trucks and SUVs at 105 Ash St in Whitby, Ontario.

Text photos of the damage to 905-730-6089 and we will reply with an estimate. In Ontario, the repair shop is your choice, not your insurer’s.

What we repair after an accident

  • Bumpers that are cracked, split or pushed in
  • Dented and creased doors, fenders, quarter panels and hoods
  • Panels that need replacing rather than repairing
  • Paint damage across one panel or several
  • Complete bodywork after a larger impact

If your vehicle was hit hard enough that it does not sit, steer or close properly, tell us when you send the photos. Some damage is easy to see and some is behind the panel.

How a collision repair works here

Estimate

Send photos or bring the car in. You get a written estimate before any work starts. If there is an insurance claim, we send your insurer what they need.

Repair

We strip the damaged area, repair or replace the panel, then prime, mask and spray to match your colour. If we find hidden damage, we call you before doing extra work.

Finish

The repair is polished and the car is reassembled and cleaned. You get updates along the way, so you always know where your repair is at.

How pricing works

Two cars with the same dent rarely cost the same to fix. The price depends on which panel is damaged, how deep the damage goes, whether the panel can be repaired or has to be replaced, and how much paint is needed to blend the repair.

So we do it in a fixed order:

  1. You send photos, or we look at the car in the shop.
  2. You get a written estimate before any work starts.
  3. If we find hidden damage once a panel is off, we call you and only continue with your OK.
Your protection under Ontario law. A repair shop must give you a written estimate before it can charge you, and the final bill cannot be more than 10% above that estimate. You can also ask for your old parts back, and your invoice has to say whether each part is new, used or reconditioned. Source: Government of Ontario — Car repair shops: your rights

Insurance claims

Most collision repairs come with a claim attached. We can send your insurer the photos and the estimate they need so the claim keeps moving while you get on with your week.

The part most drivers do not know: in Ontario you have the right to choose your own repair shop, tow operator and rental company. An insurer can suggest a shop. It cannot make you use one.

Questions about collision repair

Is my car safe to drive after a collision?

If lights, mirrors, wheels or steering are damaged, or warning lights come on, do not drive it. Have it towed. In Ontario you choose where your car is towed, so you can ask for Halif at 105 Ash St, Whitby. Not sure? Call 905-730-6089 and describe the damage.

Do I need to report the accident?

In Ontario you report a collision to a Collision Reporting Centre when combined damage is over $5,000. The Durham centre is at 650 Rossland Road East, Whitby, open daily 10 am to 6 pm. Call police instead if anyone is hurt or a driver leaves the scene.

Can I choose Halif if my insurer suggests another shop?

Yes. Ontario drivers have the right to choose their repair shop, tow operator and rental company. Your insurer can recommend a shop, but the decision is yours. Source: Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario.

How long will a collision repair take?

It depends on the damage and on how quickly parts arrive. A scuffed bumper is a much shorter job than a repair that needs new panels. We give you the expected time with your estimate and keep you updated while the car is with us.

Will you deal with my insurer?

We help with insurance claims and can send your insurer the photos and the estimate they need. Bring your claim number and your adjuster’s details when you drop the car off.
