Finding us

We are on Ash St in downtown Whitby, just off Dundas Street East — Highway 2, the main road through town.

  • From Highway 401: the Whitby exits are at Brock St and Thickson Rd. Head north to Dundas St E, then turn onto Ash St.
  • By GO train: Whitby GO is on the Lakeshore East line, south of downtown.
  • From Oshawa, Ajax or Pickering: Dundas St E runs the whole way across Durham and brings you straight into downtown Whitby.

The fastest way to get a price

You do not need to come in first. Send three photos — the whole vehicle, a close-up of the damage, and one from an angle — to 905-730-6089 by text or WhatsApp, with your name and the car’s make, model and year.
