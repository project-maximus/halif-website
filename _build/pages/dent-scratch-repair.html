Worth fixing, or leave it?

Here is the honest version. A scratch that sits only in the clear coat is cosmetic. You can live with it.

A scratch that has gone through the paint to the primer or the bare metal is different. Once salt and water reach metal, rust starts underneath the paint and spreads outwards. That small scratch becomes a panel repair a couple of winters later.

Run a fingernail across it. If your nail catches, send us a photo.

Questions about dents and scratches

How much does it cost to fix a bumper scratch?

It depends on the size, the depth and whether the panel needs paint. A light scuff on a bumper costs far less than a deep scratch across two panels. Text a photo to 905-730-6089 and we will tell you the price and whether it is worth fixing.

Is it worth fixing a small scratch?

Sometimes it is not, and we will say so. It is worth doing when the scratch has gone through the paint to the primer or metal, because that is where rust starts. If it is only in the clear coat, the answer may be different. Send a photo and ask.

How do I tell how deep a scratch is?

Run a fingernail lightly across it. If your nail catches, the scratch has gone past the clear coat. If it glides over, it is shallow. Either way a photo in daylight tells us more than a description.

Will the repair be visible afterwards?

That is the whole job. The repaired area is painted and blended into the panels beside it so your eye reads one continuous colour. Our customers’ reviews mention attention to the small details.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A single door ding or a scuffed bumper corner is a normal day’s work. Send a photo and we will tell you what it takes.
