Estimates and pricing

How do I get an estimate?

Text or WhatsApp photos of the damage to 905-730-6089, or drop by 105 Ash St in Whitby during opening hours. Send a wide shot of the car, a close-up of the damage and one from an angle. We reply with an estimate.

What does an estimate cost?

If a shop charges for an estimate, Ontario law says it has to tell you the cost in advance. You do not pay that fee if you approve the repair before the vehicle is put back together. Ask us when you send your photos and we will tell you straight away.

Can my final bill be higher than the estimate?

Not by much. In Ontario, the final cost cannot be more than 10% above your written estimate. If we find hidden damage once a panel is off, we call you first and only continue with your OK.

How much does it cost to fix a scratch or dent?

It depends on the size, the depth and whether the panel needs paint. A light scuff on a bumper costs far less than a deep scratch across two panels. Text us a photo and we will tell you the price and whether it is worth fixing.

Can I send photos by WhatsApp?

Yes. Send them to 905-730-6089 by text message or WhatsApp. Include your name and your car’s make, model and year.

After an accident

What should I do right after a crash in Whitby?

First, make sure everyone is safe and call 911 if anyone is hurt. If total damage is over $5,000, report it at the Durham Collision Reporting Centre within 48 hours. Then contact your insurer and send us photos. You choose where your car is repaired.

Where is the Durham Collision Reporting Centre?

At 650 Rossland Road East, Whitby, ON L1N 9G5. It is open every day from 10 am to 6 pm. Phone (905) 668-4157. Bring the vehicle, your licence, ownership, proof of insurance and the other driver’s details.

Source: Durham Regional Police Service

Do I need to report a small accident?

In Ontario you report a collision to the reporting centre when combined damage is over $5,000. Under that amount you do not need a police report, but you should still tell your insurer. Call police instead if anyone is hurt, a driver leaves the scene, or impairment is suspected.

Is my car safe to drive after a collision?

If lights, mirrors, wheels or steering are damaged, or warning lights come on, do not drive it. Have it towed. Not sure? Call us at 905-730-6089 and describe the damage.

Can my car be towed straight to you?

In Ontario you choose where your car is towed. Ask for Halif Auto Repair and Collision, 105 Ash St, Whitby, and confirm the tow rate before the truck hooks up. Call us on 905-730-6089 so we know it is on the way.

Insurance

Can I choose my own body shop if I claim insurance?

Yes. In Ontario you have the right to choose your repair shop, tow company and rental company. Your insurer may suggest a shop, but the choice is yours.

Source: Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario

Do you work with my insurance company?

We help with insurance claims and can send your insurer the photos and the estimate they need. Tell us who you are insured with and your claim number when you get in touch and we will take it from there.

Who pays if the accident was not my fault?

In Ontario, you usually claim damage to your car from your own insurer, even when another driver caused it. If you were not at fault, you do not pay a deductible. Coverage can differ by policy, so check with your insurer.

What do I need to bring for an insurance repair?

Your claim number, your adjuster’s name and contact details, your insurance card and the car keys. If you reported at the Collision Reporting Centre, bring that report number too.

Will using Halif cost me more than the insurer’s shop?

Ontario drivers have the right to a reasonable repair of their damaged vehicle and the right to choose the shop that does it. Your coverage and your deductible come from your policy, not from which shop you pick. If you are unsure how your claim will be settled, ask your adjuster.

Repairs and quality

Will the new paint match my car?

Matching your colour and finish is the goal on every job. We work from your vehicle’s paint code and blend the new paint into the panels beside the repair. A trade customer who sends us vehicles says the paint always comes out a perfect match and the same shade.

Can you fix rust?

Yes. We repair rust on wheel arches, rocker panels, door bottoms and other body panels on cars, trucks and SUVs. Catch it early and the repair stays smaller. Rust that has reached the parts holding the vehicle together is a different conversation, and we will tell you honestly.

Do you use new parts?

We go through the parts on your estimate before the work starts. Under Ontario rules your invoice must say whether each part is new, used or reconditioned, and you can ask for your old parts back.

Is there a warranty on your work?

By Ontario law, repair parts and labour carry at least a 90-day or 5,000 km warranty, whichever comes first. Ask us about the cover on your specific repair when you approve the estimate.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the damage and on parts. Small scratches and dents are often quicker than collision repairs that need new panels. We tell you the expected time with your estimate and keep you updated while your car is with us.
