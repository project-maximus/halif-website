Halif Auto Repair and Collision is an auto body shop at 105 Ash St in downtown Whitby, Ontario. We repair collision damage, bodywork and bumpers, paint and colour matching, dents, scratches and rust on cars, trucks and SUVs, and we help drivers with insurance claims.

Our team brings over 30 years of experience. Call or text 905-730-6089.

Who we are

We are a local shop, not a branch of a chain. The people who look at your car are the people who repair it, and George answers every one of our Google reviews himself.

That matters more than it sounds. When something needs explaining — why a panel has to be replaced instead of repaired, why the paint needs blending into the next door — you are talking to someone who was actually holding the tool.

How we work

  • You get a written estimate before any work starts. That is Ontario law and it is also just how a repair should begin.
  • If we find hidden damage, we call you. Nothing extra happens without your OK.
  • We keep you updated. You will always know where your car is at.
  • We give you the honest answer. Including when the honest answer is that a repair is not worth the money.

Where the colour match comes from

The paint booth is the part customers notice. A trade customer who sends us vehicles says the paint always comes out a perfect match and the same shade — and for a shop that does this every day, that is the compliment that counts.

A booth controls dust, airflow and temperature. That is what lets a repaired panel dry to the same depth of shine as the rest of the car, instead of looking like a patch.

While you wait

A clean, bright place to sit

In the owner’s own words: “Our waiting area is kept clean, bright, and calm so you can relax while we take care of your auto repair.”

Nobody enjoys the day their car gets damaged. The least a body shop can do is not make the rest of it worse — a tidy place to sit, a clear explanation of what is happening, and an honest answer about how long it takes.
