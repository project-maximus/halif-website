Halif Auto Repair and Collision is an auto body shop in downtown Whitby. We repair collision damage, bodywork and bumpers, paint and colour matching, dents, scratches and rust on cars, trucks and SUVs, and we help with insurance claims.

See how claims work
However the job starts

It always starts the same way

  1. You send photos or bring the car in.
  2. You get a written estimate before any work starts.
  3. If we find hidden damage, we call you before going further.
  4. We keep you updated until it is ready to collect.

Under Ontario law your final bill cannot be more than 10% above the written estimate, you can ask for your old parts back, and your invoice has to say whether each part is new, used or reconditioned.
