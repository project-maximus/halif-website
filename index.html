Before winter

Salt is already on the road

Durham Region puts salt brine down before storms and salts regional roads seven days a week through the winter. Salt keeps eating metal even after the temperature climbs back above freezing.

If paint is bubbling on a wheel arch, a rocker panel or the bottom of a door, that is rust working outwards from underneath. Catch it now and the repair stays small. Leave it and the panel usually has to be cut and rebuilt.

Common questions

Answers before you call

How do I get an estimate from Halif?

Text or WhatsApp photos of the damage to 905-730-6089, or drop by 105 Ash St in Whitby during opening hours. Send a wide shot of the car, a close-up of the damage and one from an angle. We reply with an estimate.

Can I choose my own body shop if I claim insurance?

Yes. In Ontario you have the right to choose your repair shop, tow company and rental company. Your insurer may suggest a shop, but the choice is yours. Source: Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario.

Can my final bill be higher than the estimate?

Not by much. In Ontario, the final cost cannot be more than 10% above your written estimate. If we find hidden damage once a panel is off, we call you first and only continue with your OK.

Will the new paint match my car?

Matching your colour and finish is the goal on every paint job. A trade customer who sends us vehicles says the paint always comes out a perfect match and the same shade.

Can you fix rust?

Yes. We repair rust on wheel arches, rocker panels, door bottoms and other body panels on cars, trucks and SUVs. Catch it early and the repair stays smaller.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5:30 pm. Saturday, 9 am to 2:30 pm. Closed Sunday. We are at 105 Ash St, Whitby, just off Dundas Street East in downtown Whitby.
