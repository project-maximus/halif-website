Privacy policy

What we do with the photos and details you send us, and what we never ask for.

  • Home
  • Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

We use the photos, phone number and vehicle details you send us only to prepare your repair estimate and carry out the work. We do not sell your information and we never ask for payment or card details by text message or on this website.

Who we are

Halif Auto Repair and Collision, 105 Ash St, Whitby, Ontario L1N 4B1, Canada. Phone and text: 905-730-6089.

What we collect

WhatWhy we have it
Photos of your vehicle and its damageTo work out what the repair involves and what it costs
Your name and phone numberTo reply with your estimate and keep you updated on the repair
Vehicle make, model, year and plate or VIN if you send itTo order the right parts and match your paint
Insurance claim number and adjuster details, if you have a claimTo send your insurer the estimate and photos they need

How you send it to us

Most people send photos by text message or WhatsApp to 905-730-6089. Those messages go through your own phone and messaging service, under that provider’s terms. This website does not collect or store your photos. The buttons on this site simply open your own phone app — messages, WhatsApp or the dialer — and nothing leaves your phone until you press send.

Who we share it with

  • Your insurer or adjuster, when you have a claim and we are preparing or submitting the estimate for it.
  • Parts suppliers, where we need to order the right part for your vehicle.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade your details, and we do not use them for marketing from other companies.

How long we keep it

We keep repair records, estimates and invoices for as long as we need them to support the work we have done, to answer questions about a past repair, and to meet Ontario record-keeping and tax requirements. Photos sent for an estimate that does not go ahead are kept only as long as they are useful for that enquiry.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you and your vehicle.
  • You can ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • You can ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Call or text 905-730-6089, or come and see us at 105 Ash St during opening hours.

What we never do

  • We never ask for credit card, debit card or banking details by text message, WhatsApp or through this website.
  • We never ask you to pay a deposit before you have seen a written estimate.
  • We never post photos of a customer’s vehicle publicly without asking first.

If you get a message asking for any of those and it claims to be from us, do not reply to it. Call us on 905-730-6089 and check.

This website

This site uses standard web hosting, which records ordinary technical information such as IP address and browser type in server logs. Pages that show a map embed content from Google Maps, and pages that use our web fonts load them from Google Fonts; those services apply their own privacy terms. Links to our Google listing, Instagram and WhatsApp take you to those companies’ own platforms.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Questions

Ask us. Call or text 905-730-6089, or visit 105 Ash St, Whitby, ON L1N 4B1, Monday to Friday 9 am – 5:30 pm or Saturday 9 am – 2:30 pm.
