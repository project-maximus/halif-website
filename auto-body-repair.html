Auto body and bumper repair in Whitby

Panels, doors, fenders, hoods and bumpers repaired or replaced, then painted to match.

Auto body repair is repairing or replacing the outer metal and plastic panels of a vehicle — doors, fenders, quarter panels, hoods and bumpers. Halif Auto Repair and Collision does complete bodywork on cars, trucks and SUVs at 105 Ash St in Whitby, Ontario.

Text photos of the damage to 905-730-6089 and we will tell you whether the panel can be repaired or needs replacing, and what it costs.

What we fix

  • Dented and creased doors, fenders, quarter panels and hoods
  • Cracked, split or scuffed bumper covers
  • Panels damaged by a pole, a post or another car in a parking lot
  • Panel replacement where a repair would not hold up
  • Vehicle restoration work on older cars and trucks

Not every dent is a write-off and not every dent is worth fixing. Send a photo first and we will give you a straight answer either way.

Repair or replace?

A shallow dent in a sound panel is usually worked back into shape, filled where it needs it, then primed and painted. That keeps your original panel on the car.

A panel that is torn, stretched or creased through a body line is a different job. Forcing a repair there costs more than a replacement and rarely looks right afterwards. We will tell you which one you are looking at before you spend anything.

Bumper repair in Whitby

Bumpers take most of the small damage in Durham — parking posts, shopping carts, snowbanks and low-speed taps. Modern bumper covers are plastic, so a lot of what looks like a write-off is repairable.

  • Scuffed corners and kerb rash
  • Cracks and splits in the bumper cover
  • Bumpers pushed out of line or hanging loose
  • Refinishing so the bumper matches the panels beside it

Whether a bumper is repaired or replaced depends on where the damage is and whether the mounting points behind it survived. Send a photo of the whole bumper plus a close-up and we will tell you.

How a body repair works here

Assess

We look at the panel and what is behind it, then give you a written estimate before any work starts.

Panel work

The panel is repaired back to shape or replaced, and the surface is made flat and true.

Prime and paint

We prime, mask and spray to match your colour, blending into the panels beside the repair.

Finish

The repair is polished, the car is put back together and cleaned, and you pick it up.

How pricing works

The price depends on which panel is damaged, how deep the damage goes, whether the panel is repaired or replaced, and how much paint is needed to blend it. Here is the order it happens in:

  1. Send photos, or bring the car to 105 Ash St.
  2. You get a written estimate before any work starts.
  3. If we find hidden damage once the panel is off, we call you and only continue with your OK.
Ontario rules work in your favour. A shop must give a written estimate before it can charge you, and the final bill cannot exceed that estimate by more than 10%. Your invoice must list each part as new, used or reconditioned. Source: Government of Ontario — Car repair shops: your rights

Questions about body repair

Do you repair the panel or replace it?

It depends on how far the metal has moved and how clean the damage is. A shallow dent in a sound panel is usually repaired. A panel that is torn, stretched or creased through a body line is often cheaper and stronger to replace. We tell you which one your car needs with the estimate.

Can you fix a cracked plastic bumper?

Often, yes. Plastic bumper covers can be repaired, refinished and painted back to match. Whether it is worth repairing depends on where the crack is and how the mounting points have held up. Send a photo and we will tell you.

Do you use new parts?

We go through the parts on your estimate before the work starts. Under Ontario rules your invoice must say whether each part is new, used or reconditioned, and you can ask for your old parts back.

Can you match the paint on just one panel?

Yes. Painting a single panel and blending it into the ones beside it is normal bodywork. A trade customer who sends us vehicles says the paint always comes out a perfect match and the same shade.

Do you work on trucks and SUVs?

Yes. We repair cars, trucks and SUVs.
